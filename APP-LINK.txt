CEO — Carmen Elena Organiza
============================

THE APP LINK (open this on the iPhone):
https://ermejia07.github.io/carmen-elena-organiza/

How Carmen Elena installs it on her phone:
1. Open the link above in Safari on the iPhone
2. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow, at the bottom)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (Añadir a inicio)
4. Tap Add — the CEO icon now lives on her home screen

That's it. Tap the CEO icon → scan → name it → done.
Everything stays private, on her phone. Works offline.
