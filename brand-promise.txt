THE EUGENIO STANDARD — Brand Promise (applies to every app)

🔒 Private — User data stays on their device. No tracking, no selling, no spying.
🛡️ Secure — No accounts to hack, no passwords to steal, nothing to leak.
🙂 Friendly — Warm, simple, welcoming. Built for real people.
👆 Intuitive — If you can use a phone, you can use it. No manual needed.
✨ Unique — A fresh idea, not a copy.
💡 Problem solver — It exists to make one real thing in life easier.

Trust line to reuse in descriptions, scripts, and promo copy:
"No sign-up, no account, no email. Your data stays on your phone — it's never sent
anywhere. Free, and works offline."

Spanish version used in this app's "Tu privacidad" card:
"Sin cuentas, sin registro, sin correo. Tus documentos se guardan solo en tu teléfono —
nunca se envían a ningún lado. Gratis y funciona sin internet."
